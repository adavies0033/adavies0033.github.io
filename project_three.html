Hardware Engineering at HWP-XC/RBAU Robert Bosch Pty Ltd.

Bosch’s XC division has focused on delivering software-defined vehicles with state of the art compute platforms and zonal ECU architectures. These systems integrate multiple electronic control units into large scale central vehicle computers which control data flow, power distribution, and safety critical communication across vehicle domains. Bosch’s compute solutions provide such services ranging from over-the-air updates to real time sensor fusion and coordination between powertrain, motion, and ADAS subsystems. This is how the XC team contributes to the global effort, by designing and validating leading edge ECUs and gateways, helping switch modern vehicles away from hardware-centric designs toward scalable, software driven architectures that underlie the whole digital backbone of mobility at the Clayton RBAU campus.

Regrettingly, I'm unable to disclose specific details about the projects I worked on due to confidentiality agreements with Bosch. However, I will reflect on the skills and experiences I gained during my time there. I had the opportunity to work under a Department of experienced engineers who guided me through the intricacies of hardware design, and PCB development processes. I was extremely fortunate to be involved with not only vaious stages of hardware development, but also several completely independent projects that exposed me to a multitude of engineering skills and topics. Robert Bosch Pty Ltd. produced me to the strange and wonderful world of RF design, automotive networking protocols. I worked with engineers of large control units for mutliple OEMs and learnt about the challenges of automotive compliance testing, functional safety standards, and the rigors of automotive grade hardware design. I was tasked with everthing from debugging EMI failures, to repeated thermal runs and efficiency determinations. I took part in design reviews, test plan creation, and validation testing, all while learning the importance of documentation, traceability, and teamwork in a large engineering organization. I was able to prototype, and modify several hardware designs, and see designs progress from initial concept to production ready hardware. Overall, my time at Bosch was an invaluable experience that significantly enhanced my understanding of hardware engineering and has greatly advanced my skillsets as an engineer. This position has been technically challenging, rewarding, and has provided me with a strong foundation for my future career in engineering.
